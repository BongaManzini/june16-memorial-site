# 🕯️ June 16 Soweto Memorial: Interactive 3D History Experience
### An Interactive 3D WebGL Memorial Honoring the 1976 Soweto Youth Uprising

> **Live Memorial**: [https://bongamanzini.github.io/june16-memorial-site/](https://bongamanzini.github.io/june16-memorial-site/)  
> **Built by**: Bonga Manzini (BSc Informatics student at UNISA)

---

## 🔬 About This Project

June 16 is celebrated as Youth Day across South Africa, but many young people only see it as a day off from school without knowing what the students went through in 1976.

I built this 3D web experience using Three.js and WebGL so students can walk through historical street scenes, listen to audio testimonies, and explore the timeline of the student march against the Bantu Education Act.

---

## The 11 Questions: The Facts Behind The Project

### 1. Who is this for?
- High school and university students, teachers, and anyone wanting to experience South African history visually.

### 2. Where does this focus on?
- Soweto, Johannesburg (starting at Naledi High School, Morris Isaacson, and Vilakazi Street).

### 3. Why did I build it?
- Textbook history can feel distant. Putting historical events into an interactive 3D space makes the courage of those 1976 students feel real and immediate.

### 4. How does the 3D scene work?
- Built with Three.js WebGL to create an interactive 3D memorial scene that runs smoothly in any mobile or desktop web browser without downloading an app.

### 5. When did these events happen?
- June 16, 1976 through today.

### 6. The Problem I Noticed
- Traditional museum exhibits are not accessible to students living in rural areas or small towns far from Johannesburg.

### 7. Where I Got The Data
- Truth and Reconciliation Commission (TRC) transcripts, South African History Online (SAHO) archives, and museum oral histories.

### 8. Why I Trusted This Source
- TRC transcripts provide direct first-hand accounts from surviving student leaders and families.

### 9. How I Built It
- I gathered historical photos and testimonies, mapped the route of the 1976 student march, and programmed a 3D environment with interactive info points using Three.js and React.

### 10. What I Hope To Achieve
- A free, web-accessible educational resource that schools and students can explore on their phones or laptops.

### 11. Why This Matters In Real Life
- Digital education and interactive 3D learning keep students engaged much better than static textbook pages.
